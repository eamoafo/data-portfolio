
# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

## Project Overview

Cyclistic is a fictional bike-sharing company based in Chicago, United States. Launched in 2016, the company operates a fleet of 5,824 bicycles across 692 docking stations.

Cyclistic offers three pricing plans: single-ride passes, full-day passes, and annual memberships. Customers purchasing single-ride or full-day passes are classified as casual riders, while annual subscribers are classified as members.

## Business Problem

Cyclistic's financial analysts have determined that annual members are more profitable than casual riders. The company therefore wants to increase annual memberships by converting existing casual riders.

## Business Task

Analyze historical bike-trip data to identify differences in riding behavior between casual riders and annual members. The findings will support data-driven marketing strategies aimed at increasing annual memberships.
